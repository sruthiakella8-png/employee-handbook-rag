# Nimbus Technologies — Employee Handbook

## 1. Leave Policy

### 1.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days. Any leave beyond this cap is forfeited unless special approval is granted by HR.

### 1.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 15 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year and is not encashed upon resignation.

### 1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per statutory requirements. Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

### 1.4 Leave Encashment on Resignation
Unused annual leave (up to the carried-forward cap) is encashed at the time of resignation, calculated based on the employee's last drawn basic salary. Sick leave is not eligible for encashment under any circumstance.

### 1.5 Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave balance may request unpaid leave, subject to manager and HR approval, for a maximum of 15 days per year.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

### 2.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible to work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval based on role requirements.

### 2.2 Full-Time Remote Arrangements
Full-time remote work arrangements may be granted on a case-by-case basis for roles approved by department heads, and are reviewed every 6 months.

### 2.3 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office setup allowance of ₹15,000 for employees approved for remote work exceeding 3 days per week.

### 2.4 Core Hours
All employees, whether remote or in-office, must be available during core hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST for meetings and collaboration.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement Policy

### 3.1 Travel Expenses
Employees traveling for official company business may claim economy-class airfare, standard hotel accommodation (up to ₹6,000/night), and local transportation. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of travel completion with original receipts.

### 3.2 Internet and Phone Reimbursement
Employees working remotely are eligible for a monthly internet reimbursement of up to ₹1,000, submitted via the expense portal with a copy of the bill.

### 3.3 Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment expenses up to ₹3,000 per event may be claimed with prior manager approval and itemized receipts.

### 3.4 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, alcohol (unless part of approved client entertainment), and first-class travel upgrades are not reimbursable under any circumstance.

## 4. Code of Conduct

### 4.1 Workplace Behavior
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in termination.

### 4.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or trade secrets to any external party, both during and after employment, as outlined in the signed NDA.

### 4.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, business ownership, or financial interest that may conflict with their role at the company to HR before accepting or continuing such engagements.

### 4.4 Use of Company Property
Company laptops, software licenses, and access credentials are for official use only and must be returned in good condition upon separation from the company.

## 5. Benefits

### 5.1 Health Insurance
The company provides group health insurance coverage of ₹5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children, with no waiting period for pre-existing conditions.

### 5.2 Provident Fund
Both employee and employer contribute 12% of basic salary to the Provident Fund as per statutory requirements.

### 5.3 Wellness Allowance
Employees are eligible for an annual wellness allowance of ₹8,000, reimbursable against gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health counseling sessions.

### 5.4 Learning and Development
Employees can claim up to ₹20,000 per year toward professional certifications, courses, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval and relevance to their role.

## 6. Grievance Redressal

### 6.1 Reporting a Grievance
Employees may raise workplace grievances through their reporting manager, HR business partner, or the anonymous ethics hotline available on the company intranet.

### 6.2 Resolution Timeline
HR will acknowledge a formal grievance within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 15 working days, with written communication provided to the employee at each stage.

### 6.3 Escalation
If an employee is unsatisfied with the resolution, they may escalate the matter to the Head of HR, whose decision in consultation with leadership will be considered final.

## 7. Exit Policy

### 7.1 Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 days for all full-time employees. The company may, at its discretion, approve a reduced notice period or buyout of the remaining notice period.

### 7.2 Full and Final Settlement
Final settlement, including salary dues, leave encashment, and reimbursement claims, will be processed within 45 days of the employee's last working day.

### 7.3 Exit Interview
All departing employees are required to complete an exit interview with HR prior to their last working day.
